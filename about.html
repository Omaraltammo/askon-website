Askon is a fully integrated production agency, founded to deliver technical and organizational solutions that elevate events and conferences in Syria to global standards.

OUR JOURNEY

Accumulated Expertise Setting a New Standard.

Our expertise was forged on the ground, managing diverse technical projects and events across Turkey. There, we realized that the success of any project relies not only on state-of-the-art equipment, but on meticulous planning and smart management.

We brought this experience to Syria with a clear vision: to establish a single entity combining audio engineering, visual production, and live interpretation. We provide our clients with a professional environment that ensures the success of their projects from A to Z.
